### Participation

The Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) is a not-for-profit, worldwide organization focused on improving the security of application software. Our mission is to make application security visible, so that people and organizations can make informed decisions about true application security risks. Everyone is free to participate in OWASP and all of our materials are available under a free and open software license.

Become an OWASP Member TODAY [owasp.org/membership](https://owasp.org/membership)

### Sponsors

#### How can your company sponsor OWASP Seattle

- **Venue & Hospitality Hosting**: Providing an event space, security check-in, pizza/catering, or beverages for monthly meetups.  
- **Direct Event/Chapter Sponsorship**: Financial support to cover speaker travel, AV equipment, or special events (e.g., local mini-conferences or workshops).  

#### What recognition does a sponsor receive?

Sponsors of the OWASP Seattle Chapter typically receive a blend of digital exposure, physical event branding, direct talent access, and community goodwill.  

Because OWASP operates under non-profit 501(c)(3) guidelines, sponsorship benefits focus on brand visibility and relationship-building rather than aggressive sales pitches.  

**Digital & Branding Exposure**

- **Official Web & Wiki Recognition**: Logo, company description, and link featured on the official OWASP Seattle chapter page and meet-up platform listings.  
- **Slide & Meeting Deck Placement**: Inclusion on the intro/welcome slides displayed on-screen before and between presentation sessions.  
- **Social Media & Newsletter Mentions**: Dedicated shout-outs on OWASP Seattle’s social media channels (LinkedIn, Slack) and email updates to local members.  
- **Supporter Badges**: Permission to use official OWASP Supporter logos on the company's own site, press releases, or recruitment materials.  

**In-Person Event Privileges**

- **Opening Remarks / Welcome Pitch**: A short (2–5 minute) intro slot at the beginning of a meetup to welcome attendees, introduce the company, and share open job roles.  
- **Exhibit or Networking Table:** Dedicated table space in the networking area to distribute swag, display banners, share product literature, and chat with attendees.  
- **Venue Hosting Branding**: If hosting the event at company headquarters, sponsors get direct foot traffic, signage opportunities, and a chance to showcase their office culture.  

**Talent Access & Recruitment Benefits**

- **Direct Access to Attendees**: Face-to-face interaction with active application security engineers, security architects, CISOs, and developers.  
- **Job Announcements**: Dedicated shout-outs for open roles during the meeting, as well as job postings on the local chapter's Slack or discussion channels.  
- **Opt-in Attendee Connection**: Opportunity to collect contact information (e.g., via raffle entries or sign-up sheets at a sponsor table).  

**Content & Thought Leadership Opportunities**

- **Technical Speaking Slots**: Opportunity to nominate non-vendor, educational talks (subject to chapter board approval to maintain community pitch-free standards).  
- **Workshop Co-Hosting**: Sponsoring or co-facilitating specialized hands-on sessions, Capture the Flag (CTF) competitions, or security hackathons.  

To protect the neutrality of the community, pure sales presentations are prohibited, but technical expertise and hiring efforts are actively encouraged.
